How to Play

Game mechanics, UI guide, boosts, passes, and everything else explained

🐠

The Basics

Be Fish is an idle fish collection game on Roblox. You control a fish in the fish tank. Eat food pellets and other fish to grow larger and collect fish in your net. The fish you have equipped determines your Growth, Speed, and XP multiplier stats. Higher-tier and rarer fish have dramatically better stats.

There are 60 unique species, each in 5 tiers, for 300 total collectibles. Tiers progress from Normal up to the incredibly rare Shadow: You only ever get normal-tier fish in your net — to get higher tiers, you need to craft them by collecting multiples of the same species. The progression is:

Normal → 50× Golden → 50× Rainbow → 50× Glowing → 50× Shadow
💡 Crafting 50 Normal turns into 1 Golden, then you start with 0 golden of that fish. Next you need 50 more Golden to craft into a Rainbow version, and so on. Want the exact cumulative totals for each tier? See Game Mechanics → Merging & Tiers.

Fish Stats

Every fish has two core stats displayed as a bar. The Fish Dex on this wiki converts those bars to a 1–100 numerical scale for easy comparison. Stats shown as ??? haven't been unlocked yet.

Size gain
Growth
How much your fish grows each time it eats a food pellet or other fish. Higher = get bigger numbers above your fish while in the tank.
Movement
Speed
How fast your fish moves around the tank. One of the most important stats. Higher = chase others and escape predators.
XP
XP Multiplier
Determines how fast you collect fish in your net by eating pellets/food in the tank.
1 in ???
Rarity
How rare this a fish is, based on the 1 in xx number value. Higher rarity = much better stats.
💡 While growing big can feel rewarding, the real endgame is collecting fish for your FishDex. This is a grinding game. More time spent in the tank = more nets and better fish. Collecting fish also earns permanent bonuses from your FishDex— see Game Mechanics → Fishdex Collection Bonus.

Boosts

Boosts are temporary power-ups purchased with Robux. They last for a set duration, then expire. You can use more than one, but it only increases the time, not the bonus. Access them from the Boosts button at the bottom-right of the game screen. All boosts stack with passes and Fishdex bonuses.

Passes

Passes are permanent upgrades purchased with Robux. 1 time purchase that never expire and are always active — unlike boosts, which are temporary.

🍀
Lucky
Permanently +100% Luck. Stacks with all other Luck sources.
⏩
Fast XP
Permanently +50% XP for your in tank XP bar. Inceases your rate of collecting nets.
🎣
Double Loot
Get 2 nets every time you your xp bar fills, instead of 1! Doubles fish collection rate.
🧲
Food Magnet
Increases your circle of food collection radius. You can see this transparent circle around your fish. Highly beneficial.
📈
Double Growth
Gain 2× size from everything you eat. Stacks with your fish's Growth stat and Growth Boost.
💡 If you're only buying one pass, Double Loot gives the best return — it permanently doubles every net you get. If you plan to play for any significant amount of time, all the passes are woth getting.

Gems

Gems are the premium currency in Be Fish, purchased with Robux. Their primary use is to instantly skip Treasure Chest unlock timers, giving you immediate access to the fish inside and permanently increasing Luck. Pricing varies per account, time periods, and geographic region.
